## Three complete solution candidates

### A — 证据审阅 (selected)

- Narrative intent: keep the confirmed five topics and give every page a judgment that can be checked; results and their limitations lead to a bounded next step.
- Mode: custom. Basis: pyramid. Behavior: each page starts with a supported assertion, follows with the required evidence, and closes with its interpretation boundary or next decision; the five-page source order is preserved.
- Visual style: custom. Bases: swiss-minimal for precise alignment, large flat fields and whitespace; data-journalism for source lines, numerical annotation and the result-page sidebar. Sans-serif typography remains fixed by the accepted source plan.
- Palette: background #FFFFFF; secondary_bg #EFF3F7; primary #142B45; accent #C27B22; secondary_accent #008C95; body_text #203247.
- Typography: heading/body Microsoft YaHei, Arial; body 28 px; title 42 px. Icons: none; step labels and functional geometry provide orientation.
- Image rendering candidate: custom, basis blueprint. Crisp uniform schematic lines, flat depth, no material texture, and the deck's semantic accents describe technical relationships. Mood resembles a carefully annotated engineering review sheet. Rendering is retained as a candidate only; no AI image source is selected.
- Source/resource recommendation: none for raster/AI/web assets. Reconstruct conceptual diagrams as native objects; the pressure half-amplitude widths are a native editable chart built from saved numerical results. Existing PNG pages are reference-only and are never flattened into the delivered slide.
- Reading mode: balanced, with presenter explanation and source-rich notes. Page budget: 5. Notes enabled; all motion disabled; narration disabled; continuous execution; brief spec; no spec-refinement gate.
- Rhythm: anchor / dense / dense / dense / anchor. Result page is the focal evidence page.

### B — 研究演进

- Narrative intent: emphasize how successive modeling discoveries narrowed the research question from architecture to a falsifiable single-point goal.
- Mode: custom, basis narrative. Behavior: frame the original question, show the tool-building response, establish the current configuration, reveal the spatial-selectivity tension, and end with the next evidence threshold.
- Visual style: custom, basis editorial. Behavior: an asymmetric running milestone rail, large chapter numerals, column rules and occasional pull statements carry the chronology; fixed YaHei/Arial typography uses weight and scale for editorial contrast.
- Palette: background #FFFFFF; secondary_bg #EFF3F7; primary #142B45; accent #C27B22; secondary_accent #008C95; body_text #203247.
- Typography: heading/body Microsoft YaHei, Arial; body 28 px; title 42 px. Icons: none.
- Image rendering candidate: custom, basis blueprint. Sharp schematic contours and lightweight annotations sit on flat white material without texture or perspective. Mood resembles a research notebook redrawn for a laboratory review.
- Source/resource recommendation: none; conceptual figures native and numerical summaries editable. Reading mode balanced; five pages; notes enabled; motion and narration disabled; continuous; brief spec.

### C — 验证路线

- Narrative intent: organize every result around what it enables, what it cannot establish, and which validation dependency must be satisfied next.
- Mode: custom, basis instructional. Behavior: define the evidence layers, explain the three tool lines, distinguish software and physical validation, use the result page as a worked counterexample, and close with ordered acceptance gates.
- Visual style: custom, basis swiss-minimal. Behavior: large horizontal evidence layers, strong alignment, numbered gates and sparse one-sided outlines clarify prerequisites; a single shared path changes emphasis by page job.
- Palette: background #FFFFFF; secondary_bg #EFF3F7; primary #142B45; accent #C27B22; secondary_accent #008C95; body_text #203247.
- Typography: heading/body Microsoft YaHei, Arial; body 28 px; title 42 px. Icons: none.
- Image rendering candidate: custom, basis blueprint. Uniform strokes, flat schematic objects and restrained semantic accents clarify cause and dependency without shading or texture. Mood resembles an experimental acceptance diagram on a clean lab whiteboard.
- Source/resource recommendation: none; functional diagrams and numerical chart are editable. Reading mode balanced; five pages; notes enabled; motion and narration disabled; continuous; brief spec.

## Selection rationale

Choose A because the audience needs a credible progress account and a clear reading of mixed numerical evidence. It retains the supplied five-page narrative while giving the negative findings adequate weight. B overemphasizes chronology and C would spend more of the short talk teaching the workflow.

## Final production decision under delegated authority

- Output: editable PPTX; all visible content originates in page SVG. Windows Microsoft PowerPoint is the target.
- Reading mode balanced; exactly five slides; body 28 px; title 42 px; subtitle/lead 30 px; annotation 20 px; footnote 16 px; data 40 px; display 52 px; index 22 px.
- Free design with flat PowerPoint structure. No raster, web or AI acquisition. No icon pool.
- Notes: enabled, with full evidence paths, manual revisions/pages, sources and scope qualifiers. Total talk approximately 9 minutes.
- Custom animations, transitions and narration: disabled. Generation continuous. Spec depth brief. Refinement disabled.
- Preserve source facts and all material limits; historical test counts are not rerun or added together.
